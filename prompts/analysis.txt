━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
KEY METRICS TO EXAMINE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Views: Total eyeballs on content
- Impressions: How often thumbnails were shown
- CTR: How well thumbnails and titles convert (impressions → clicks)
- Avg View Percentage: How much of the video viewers watch
- Avg Watch Time: How long viewers stay engaged
- Subscribers Gained: Growth indicator
- Traffic Sources: Where viewers are coming from
